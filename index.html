    How to Use PulseLift 3D

    STEP 1

    🎯 Hover Any Muscle to See Its Name

    To keep the 3D display clean and uncluttered, muscle labels stay hidden until you need them. Move your cursor directly over any muscle on the 3D body (or hover a muscle pill badge) to spotlight that muscle and pop up its exact anatomical name and role!

    STEP 2

    🔄 360° Orbit & Studio Rep Scrubber

    Click & drag left/right on any 3D model to inspect spinal alignment and joint angles from 360°. Use quick presets (45°, Side, Front, Back, ↻ 360°) or click ⤢ 360° Studio to use 0.5x Slow-Mo and scrub the rep from 0% to 100%.

    STEP 3

    📅 Alternating 2-Day Full-Body Split

    Switch between Day A (Goblet Squat, Floor Press, Bent-Over Row, Core Decompression) and Day B (RDL, Overhead Press, Reverse Lunge, Spinal Hang). Perform 3–4 sessions per week with 1 rest day between workouts.

    STEP 4

    🏋️ Log Weights (kg), Reps & Set Pills

    Enter your dumbbell weight (kg) and completed reps for Sets 1, 2, and 3. Click Set 1 / Set 2 / Set 3 (or the ✓ button) to mark sets complete—everything saves automatically to localStorage. Click Save Workout Log to archive your session in History.

    STEP 5

    ⏱️ Auto-Rest Timer & 8-Bit Chime

    Checking off a set automatically starts the recommended recovery countdown (90s for compounds, 60s for upper/lunges, 3m for decompression/hang). When the timer hits 00:00, it flashes and plays a Web Audio 8-bit victory chime.

    STEP 6

    📱 Offline PWA & Clinical View

    Click ☀️/🌙 on any 3D viewport to toggle between Dark Studio and White Clinical Ecorché mode. Because PulseLift 3D uses a Service Worker, it works 100% offline and can be installed as an app on your phone or desktop.
